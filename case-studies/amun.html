DISCOVERY·WEB DESIGN·CLIENT

AmunPersonal Protection

A complete digital experience for a private personal protection firm.A website redesign focused on discretion, clearer service positioning, and a consultation-first experience.

2026 · Client Project · Sole Designer

production note · this hero image was developed using generative AI from client-provided photography

OPPORTUNITY
01 / the opportunity

A clearer digital presence

The original single-page website combined three distinct services within one experience. The redesign needed to clarify those offerings, establish a more consistent visual identity, and create a direct path toward qualified consultations.

1 page→3 service lines→1 consultation path
DISCOVERY
02 / discovery & strategy

Understanding the Business

Discovery focused on the business’s audience, services, and priorities, establishing a direction built around professionalism, discretion, and qualified consultations.

three strategic priorities

Clarity

Distinguish the three services and explain what prospective clients can expect.

Discretion

Respect confidentiality through restrained language, imagery, and geographic information.

Qualification

Prioritize serious inquiries through an upfront, paid consultation process.

STRUCTURE
03 / ux strategy & information architecture

A clearer path from services to consultation

The original single-page structure became a homepage and three dedicated service pages: Personal Protection, Protected Transportation, and Safe Spaces.

Each offering gained room for clearer information while maintaining a consistent path to consultation.

Refining the Service Experience

Early wireframes presented service scenarios as image-based cards. The final design replaced these with a simpler, text-focused layout and refined supporting content to communicate each scenario more clearly.

Establishing Credibility

The original website included information about professional training, which the client wanted to highlight more prominently. I developed a dedicated EPI section with clearer hierarchy, supporting credentials, and a stronger visual presentation.

Keeping Consultation Central

Consultation remained the primary action throughout the website. A consistent call to action leads to a dedicated page outlining the consultation fee, expectations, and inquiry process.

IDENTITY
04 / visual identity & art direction

Exploring the Visual Direction

Two mood boards explored contrasting interpretations of the brand: one understated and discreet, the other darker and more commanding.

These directions established a starting point for client alignment and informed the visual identity as the design evolved.

Refining the visual language

The existing logo was retained and refined for its new presentation, while typography, color, imagery, and interface details came together to establish a more distinctive and cohesive visual identity.

Creating consistency through imagery

Client-provided photography was reframed, color graded, and edited to create a consistent visual presentation while accounting for the project’s confidentiality requirements.

BUILD
05 / design implementation & responsive development

From design to a working website

I translated the designs into a responsive Framer website, refining reusable components, layouts, and interactions across screen sizes.

EXPERIENCE

Project reflection

Amun reinforced the importance of balancing design direction with evolving client needs. Clearer approval milestones, earlier asset collection, and moving into development sooner would streamline future projects without compromising design quality.

06 / the finished experience

Bringing everything together

The finished website brings together three dedicated service pages, a refined visual identity, and a consistent consultation path, creating a cohesive experience with a clear next step for prospective clients.

Background.

Before design, I was painting. That's where I first learned to notice when something feels off. Spacing, balance, hierarchy. I usually catch it before I can explain why.

I spent almost a decade in hospitality. High volume, high pressure, a lot of reading people quickly and staying calm when things went sideways. That's not a career change footnote. It's the same muscle behind good client work and good research, just trained somewhere else first.

Outside of work I'm usually gaming, swimming, or planning the next trip. I like anything that mixes play with problem solving, which is probably why design stuck.

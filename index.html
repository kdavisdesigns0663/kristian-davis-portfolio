BIO

The Way I Work.

I don't believe great products begin with polished interfaces. They begin with understanding the people they're built for.

Hi, I'm Kristian Davis. Long before I found UX, I was fascinated by why some experiences just felt right. Painting taught me balance, composition, and visual hierarchy. Nearly a decade in hospitality taught me to read people, adapt quickly, and notice what often goes unsaid. Today, those same instincts shape every project I take on.

Design is only valuable when it solves the right problem. I enjoy asking the questions that uncover what's really happening beneath the surface, then turning those insights into products that feel simple, thoughtful, and effortless to use.

Outside of work you'll usually find me swimming, gaming, or planning my next trip. I'm endlessly curious about how things work, why people make the choices they do, and how small details shape larger experiences. That curiosity doesn't stop when I leave my desk.

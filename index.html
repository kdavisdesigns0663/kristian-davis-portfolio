BIO

The thinking behind the work.

Hi, I'm Kristian Davis. Long before I found UX, I was fascinated by why some experiences just felt right. Painting taught me balance, composition, and visual hierarchy. Nearly a decade in hospitality taught me to read people, adapt quickly, and notice what often goes unsaid.

Today, I bring those instincts into every project. I believe design is only valuable when it solves the right problem, so I enjoy asking the questions that uncover what's happening beneath the surface and turning those insights into products that feel simple, thoughtful, and intuitive.

Outside of work you'll usually find me swimming, gaming, or planning my next trip. I'm endlessly curious about how people think and how small details shape the experiences we remember.
